[size=5]Vault Admin[/size]

A debug and creative panel for Fallout Shelter, built out of the game's own widgets so it looks
like it belongs there. Four pages: your resources, everything the game can give you, a bench for
building dwellers and animals, and a page of vault-wide overrides.

No Harmony patches. Everything goes through the game's own methods.

[size=4]What it does[/size]

[b]RESOURCES[/b] — every resource the vault holds, with the game's icons and its own caps. Give a
set amount or fill to the cap. Lunchboxes, Mr Handys and pet carriers are offered separately,
because the game hands those over by a different route.

[b]ITEMS[/b] — every weapon, outfit and piece of junk in the game, each with its real name and the
game's own description; every pet, one row per animal per grade; rolled dwellers of each rarity;
and the named dwellers the game ships, each with their own portrait. Searchable, and sortable by
rarity or by any single SPECIAL stat.

[b]WORKSHOP[/b] — build a dweller from a name, a gender, a rarity, a level, seven SPECIAL values,
an appearance and a set of gear, with a live figure of that dweller standing beside the fields as
you change them. A second bench does the same for animals, including which bonus they carry and how
strong it is.

[b]OVERRIDES[/b] — full health, full happiness, revive the dead, level 50, ten in every stat,
finish every pregnancy, grow every child, finish every training, unlock every recipe, and the three
resources to their caps. Anything that cannot be undone asks before it does it.

[b]Staff the whole vault by ability.[/b] Every post is cleared and the vault laid out from empty,
in seven ranks: production first, then the clinic and laboratory, the gyms, the crafting rooms, the
vault door, the living quarters, and last the rooms that want a body and do nothing with it.
Training rooms take your [i]worst[/i] at that stat, since a dweller already at ten learns nothing in
a gym. Children, the dead, anyone in the wasteland and anyone still queued at the door are left
alone.

[b]Dress everyone for the job they are doing.[/b] Which stat matters to a dweller is a property of
the room they stand in, so each one is given the outfit in your storage that adds most to the stat
their room runs on. The vault door takes the best coat outright and the living quarters take
charisma.

[b]Standing rules, kept per vault[/b] — incidents off, Bottle and Cappy away, rushing that never
fails, and a population limit. Turning incidents off in the vault you are experimenting with does
not turn them off in the one you are playing properly. The limit's RESET hands it back to your
rooms, so building another living quarters raises it on its own.

[size=4]Requirements[/size]

[list]
[*]Fallout Shelter 2.6.0 on PC (Steam)
[*]BepInEx 5.4.x, Unity Mono, x64
[/list]

[size=4]Installation[/size]

Extract into the game folder, so the DLL lands in [b]BepInEx\plugins[/b].

Then open [b]BepInEx\config\ovolo.falloutshelter.vaultadmin.cfg[/b] and set [b]Enabled = true[/b].
The mod ships switched off on purpose: nothing is read, drawn or bound until you turn it on.

Open the panel with the button in the top left, under the dwellers button, or with [b]F8[/b].

[size=4]What it leaves behind[/size]

It writes to your save the way the game does. Anything you grant or create is yours and stays,
including after the mod is removed — those are real items and real dwellers made through the game's
own calls.

The standing rules are held in memory while the mod runs and put back the way they were found when
you switch it off. Your own settings live in the config file and nowhere else.

Removing the DLL removes the mod. Your save keeps everything it was given.

[size=4]A word of warning[/size]

This is a creative and debug tool. It will hand you two hundred of anything and level your whole
vault in one press, and it does not ask whether you wanted the game to still be a game afterwards.
Several of its actions cannot be undone; those ask first. Back up your save before the first run —
they live in [b]%LocalAppData%\FalloutShelter[/b].

[size=4]Compatibility[/size]

No Harmony patches, so it does not fight other mods for the same methods. It reads and writes the
vault through the game's own calls, which means another mod changing the same value — a population
limit, most likely — is the two of you writing to one place. Set the limit in one mod or the other,
not both.
